# Marine conservation integration — 2026-10-19

The current Explorer offers one Marine Conservation Areas family with exactly
Assignment, Count and Coverage. Assignment includes both the producer's designated
memberships and its proposed/interim reference memberships. The separate reference
family is hidden only when this combined view is available. Older catalogs retain
their separate reference until the replacement exists. No old counts or coverage
are added in the browser.

The retained reference records are Race Rocks Area of Interest (DFO proposed /
under assessment point) and Gulf Islands National Park Reserve of Canada's interim
marine zone (CPCAD PA_OECM_DF=3, STATUS=3). These are source inventory statuses, not a
fresh legal assessment. Race Rocks has no inferred polygon or area. Gulf Islands'
designated and interim zones share a parent site ID; their footprints and statuses
must remain distinct.

`scripts/conservation_input.py` appends a browser-only membership view in
`marine_conservation_assignment__feature_memberships_v1`, with its own paired
count/status support. The exposed feature ID stays
`GOVERNANCE__marine_conservation_areas__feature_memberships_v1` (plus the existing
dynamic prefix where applicable), preserving saved core Assignment selections.
Reference display keys qualify the original site ID by status:
`conservation_reference:proposed:<site ID>` or
`conservation_reference:interim:<site ID>`. The original IDs remain in paired
`sourceRecordContext.site_identity`, and source records/part IDs are unchanged.
This is a presentation distinction, not a new site deduplication claim.

Legend/assignment labels explicitly show **Proposed:** or **Interim:**. Cell popups
mark them as reference-only and excluded from designated Count and Coverage.
Insights calls the browser support count **Assignment entries**, because one site
can have both a designated entry and an interim entry. All source names, IDs,
PA/OECM/provider classes, designation types and legal mechanisms remain inspectable
and exported. Existing overlap stripes, stable colors and bounded scrolling remain.

Designated Count and Coverage still read their original producer columns, unchanged.
Coverage uses polygon unions divided by full H3 area in EPSG:6933; percent display
is `fraction × 100`, with exact raw fractions, nulls and conversion in export.
Partial no-hit support stays unknown. Proposed/interim references are never added
to designated totals. This is not a collection of legally equivalent MPAs or
complete current law. All stored Governance/Human/Meteorology products remain
byte-identical in this follow-up.

At R6/R8, designated sites remain 203/202. Combined Assignment has 205/204 distinct
site/status display entries across 929/21,973 cells, compared with designated
Assignment's 929/21,923 cells. The two reference identities occupy 24/163 cells.
These display-entry totals are not unique physical-site or designated-site counts.

Saved separate-reference, interim and AOI point-count choices now restore combined
Assignment; saved Count/Coverage and unrelated selections are unchanged. A startup
encoding bound to current source files but lacking the combined view is rejected
and regenerated by the local reader. The live endpoint is
`http://127.0.0.1:5173/local-data/seascape/catalog.json`; the historical public seed
is not the current served catalog.

Text-copy buttons are restored for all feature dropdowns, including conservation,
tribal and legacy refuge choices. Friendly labels remain unchanged. A check mark
and accessible success message confirm copying without a visible Governance
success banner; failures stay visible. Copying never selects a feature or closes
the menu. Both left and comparison selectors share this behavior.

Validation: 93 focused DOM/TypeScript checks, 31 Python adapter/live-reader checks,
typecheck, lint and production build passed. Independent HTTP readback compared
all combined memberships, records, parts, counts and statuses and the original
components/designated Count/Coverage at R6/R8. All 49 captured product/source file
